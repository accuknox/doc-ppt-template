## About

## VS One

VS One is a leading Information Technology related products and solutions Distributor, located in Sri Lanka. Established in 2019, we combine our excellence and expertise to supply our partners with globally acclaimed products and solutions. Since our humble beginnings, we have been able to expand into one of the country’s foremost IT-related products and solutions  suppliers within a very short period of time, through our unwavering commitment to excellence in everything we do.

Our company core values, integrity and reliability have paved the pathway to our success since our inception. The breadth of partners that we have achieved over these years further attests to our ethical business module and our commitment to make a change in the industry.

Equipped with a team of qualified industry professionals with years of experience behind them, we have been able to satisfy the demands of numerous partner channels. We believe in long-term relations with our partners and we enjoy helping them to grow their businesses. Today we are backed with a partner ecosystem of more than 200 partners, located around the island.

With the rapid transformation of almost all the industries to the digitalized economy, we have identified that there is a vacuum created within the information technology industry. Our vision is to fill this vacuum and enable the customers in the remote locations of the country to access the global brands and industry-leading solutions. Our ever-growing partner network is handled by industry experts, those who are geared with the mission of delivering our products to the end customers within various geo-locations.

Our offerings go beyond supplying IT-related products and solutions . Our team is known for delivering unmatched customer support, technical consultancy, industry knowledge and training that enable our partners to offer an improved service to the customers. Our continuous assistance helps them to overcome their business challenges and reach greater heights in their industry.

![](https://www.vsone.lk/wp-content/themes/vsone/assets/img/image.png)

Our vision is to create a technology-enabled digital infrastructure system within the country across various sectors.

We strive to deliver optimum value through the seamless distribution of IT services and products of the best global brands.

We endeavor to empower all the communities by creating new opportunities for a digitally-enabled future.
